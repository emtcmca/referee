## VERDICT: **NOT VERIFIED** · BLOCKING: **yes**

The contract fails on its second clause, decisively, and therefore on its first. The seven agents did
not write to a shared vocabulary. They wrote to **three incompatible manuscript-id namespaces, three
incompatible section-id sets, three incompatible rubric-criterion sets, three incompatible score
scales, two incompatible `verifyQuote` signatures, two incompatible refusal-code sets, two
incompatible persisted-state shapes, and two incompatible event buses.** An implementer opening 02,
03, 04 and 05 in sequence does not have "no further design decisions" left; they have roughly two
dozen, several of which are architectural.

This is not a style verdict. The single most damaging item, stated plainly: **`03`'s
`assert_finding` handler is written against a `verifyQuote` return shape that `04` — the owner of
`verifyQuote` — does not produce. As written, every finding in the build is refused.** That is the
demo.
